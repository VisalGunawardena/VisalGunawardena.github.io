Niyara: a single-pass paddy bund maker.

My final-year project. I lead Team ACE. Niyara is an attachment for the Vikyno RV125-2 two-wheel tractor that trims weeds, gathers and applies mud and compacts the bund in one pass.

  • My partMud gathering and application, power transmission, tractor interface
  • TargetsUnder 60 kg, at least 10 m/min, about LKR 200,000, buildable in a local workshop
  • MethodPugh matrix, SolidWorks, every calculation checked independently
  • Field workPaddy farmers and engineers at the Farm Mechanization Research Centre, Anuradhapura
  • AlsoEntered in InnoJet 3.0, Kandy Conference 2026

Twelve concepts from the concept round.

Real CAD · chosen with a Pugh matrix · hover to spin

Independent project · Python · Three.js · Oct 2026

BundLink moves water over the bund by itself.

A power-free siphon between paddy plots. A float ball protects the upper field's water; a float and rocker stop the flow when the lower field is full. Chain the units and a whole tract fills and holds.

  • Sized with a Python model of pipe flow, valve forces and float buoyancy, 50–90 mm pipe
  • No cut bunds, no night watering
Team Shear Force · Top 10 · AgriTech Startup Spark 2026

A precise, low-cost hormone doser for pineapple growers.

A calibrated syringe on a T-junction, switched between reservoir and nozzle by two valves. One cycle delivers one measured dose of flower-induction hormone.

  • 21 concepts, a 17-criterion decision matrix, FMEA and a patent landscape review
  • Business plan for a precision-agriculture equipment company

PeakTEA: wet leaf, honest weight

Wet green tea leaf inflates the weight factories pay for. We compared impedance, microwave, capacitance and centrifugal methods for measuring surface water at intake, and modelled the impedance method in MATLAB/Simulink.

Agricultural sensing research · 2026

Perez Leather Artisans

Modelled a leather-goods production process in Tecnomatix Plant Simulation to find inefficiencies and resource gaps, then optimised it for cost, waste, productivity and quality.
